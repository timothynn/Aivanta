# Veyntis brand mark

A minimalist geometric V: two converging rails suggesting systems coming together, with a narrow inner channel representing intelligence and integration.

Color palette: obsidian `#091218`, deep graphite `#11251f`, emerald `#75ddb8`, pale `#f0f7f4`.

Use a simple, iconic mark scalable from 16px favicon to large signage. No animal silhouette, gradient-heavy tech clichés, aircraft wings or robot faces. An abstract systems mark should be recognizable without the wordmark.

Production SVG artwork lives in `public/logo-mark.svg`, with `public/favicon.svg` and `public/og-image.svg`. The brand is independent of the founder's personal alter ego.
