# Aivanta Proof & Trust Layer

## Purpose

The proof layer is designed to increase credibility without inventing customers, testimonials, performance claims, or proprietary client work.

## Content model

### Illustrative scenarios

Use clearly labeled scenarios to show the kinds of systems and workflows Aivanta can improve:

- AI-assisted document review
- AI workflow assistant
- Enterprise knowledge assistant

These are demonstrations of capability, not claims of completed client engagements.

### Measurement

Frame outcomes as metrics to establish and measure during a pilot:

- Workflow cycle time
- Search effort
- Work completed or steps assisted
- Accuracy, usefulness, grounding, and consistency

Do not publish numerical improvements until they are supported by real project evidence.

### Integration credibility

Show that Aivanta is designed to work with existing application layers, APIs, databases, documents, AI services, and security controls rather than requiring a wholesale platform replacement.

### Responsible AI

Public positioning should emphasize:

- Human oversight
- Grounded answers
- Controlled agent actions
- Evaluation and monitoring

### Client proof

Only add testimonials, logos, case studies, quantified outcomes, or client names when they are real and appropriate to publish. Prefer client-approved wording and evidence.

## Conversion journey

Illustrative scenario → Transformation Assessment → Personalized starting point → Contact form → Consultation

## Future expansion

When genuine client evidence exists, add:

1. Named case studies
2. Quantified before/after metrics
3. Client-approved testimonials
4. Industry-specific examples
5. Architecture or implementation highlights that are safe to publish
